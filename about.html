Who we are

A professionally managed spice house rooted in Kerala

Established in 2009, Formosa International is a well-established export group supplying premium Indian spices and agro products to a growing customer base worldwide.

Our story

Long-term partnerships, built on strong corporate ethics

We aspire to achieve long-term business relationships with our clients on the basis of strong corporate ethics. We are committed to maintaining high quality standards for the products we supply, and we always adhere to the committed delivery schedule.

The company's ability to meet customer requirements expeditiously and at competitive prices has been its major strength — helping us gain a loyal and growing customer base in the international market. We keep a close watch on the changing market and customer preferences, and work to reflect the latest trends in our products and services.

  • Strong corporate ethics
  • Competitive pricing
  • Committed delivery schedules
  • Growing global customer base
